## 5. CONCEPT, "**Context availability is per key.** A" and MENTAL MODEL, "Before a runner is assigned, the" through "The difference between the last two"; DIAGRAM, "And the second picture: three moments."

**Picture.** The second drawing of the DIAGRAM section as a timeline with three stations: "workflow file" → "GitHub Actions service" → "runner", and inside the runner the shell.

1. `service`: `on:`, `jobs.<id>.if`, `strategy.matrix` stop at the service: "evaluated before a runner is assigned". Context chips allowed here: `github`, `needs`, `vars`, `inputs`; refused, greyed: `matrix`, `steps`, `env`, `secrets`.
2. `runner`: `steps[*].if`, `with:`, `env:`, `run: ${{ ... }}` stop at the runner: "evaluated per step, on the runner, BEFORE the shell starts". A step-level `if` may read `matrix`, `steps`, `env`; `secrets` stays grey.
3. `shell`: `run: echo "$NAME"` reaches the shell: "expanded by the shell, at run time".
4. `boundary`: a line between 2 and 3 labelled "a security boundary": on the left, the value is pasted "into the script text"; on the right, it is "handed to the shell as data".

The mail-merge analogy can sit on step 2: a letter with a field that is already filled in when the recipient, "the shell", receives it.

## 7. CONCEPT, "Where each is defined: `env` in" through "Masking: `env` no, `vars` no,", the quiz, and "B, an empty string. Three behaviors"

**Picture.** Three containers side by side, filled one row per paragraph.

| | `env` | `vars` | `secrets` |
|---|---|---|---|
| defined | "in the workflow file, at workflow, job or step level" | "settings of the organization, the repository or an environment" | the same settings |
