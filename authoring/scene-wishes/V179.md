# Scene wishes for V179: Design review briefing: branching model, governance, CI and security policy for a described company

Spots are given as section heading plus the first six words of the paragraph in `video/scripts/V179-*.md`. Every label is copied from the script.

Placed in this pass with today's library: nothing. The only commit graph in the script is the output of `git log --simplify-by-decoration`, whose edges are not parent edges, so the `graph` scene would draw a history the repository does not have. The video also draws no conclusion from the evidence on purpose ("no conclusion is drawn on screen"): no picture may interpret the numbers of the demo for the viewer, and no picture may show a filled-in design.

## 1. A name answers none of the four questions

- **Spot.** HOOK, "An auditor asks that question, and".
- **Picture.** One chip in the middle, the name of a model left blank (a grey chip with the words "a name"), and four questions around it that stay unanswered.
- **Steps.**
  1. `question`: the on-screen question "Which branching model do you follow?" and the chip "a name".
  2. `nothing`: four cards arrive one at a time, each with a question mark: "how long branches live", "which way fixes travel", "who may move which ref", "what was in the last patch release".
  3. `write-down`: the chip fades; three lines are written in its place: "the refs", "the rules", "the fix direction".
- **Return.** INTERVIEW QUESTION, "Answer out loud, in about five", at the words "what you show instead": step `write-down` again (this is where the loop from the hook is closed aloud).

- **Steps.**
  1. `parts`: the segments grow in the order of the table, each with its label and number: "Evidence 15", "Branching and release model 20", "Governance 15", "CI design 15", "Security policy 15", "Reproducibility 10", "Migration 10".

## 5. The bridge: an objection is a load

- **Spot.** MENTAL MODEL, "Think of the design as a", then "Where the picture breaks: a bridge".
- **Picture.** A simple truss bridge. Each member is a claim.
- **Steps.**
  1. `members`: three members with their loads: "a patch release that contains only fixes", "a secret that can't arrive", "a model that can be named five years later".
  2. `load`: a weight labelled "an objection" is lowered onto one member.
  3. `holds`: under that member two supports appear, "a reason" and "an enforcing layer": it holds.
  4. `nothing`: a second member rests on a card "that is best practice"; the card vanishes and the member sags: "it has nothing underneath it".
  5. `breaks`: where the picture breaks: a member is swapped for a new one with a note "what the amendment breaks elsewhere"; caption "in the defence a member you replace isn't" a defect.
