# Batch 2 (V034 to V066): finishing report

Written 2026-10-07 by the finishing inspector of batch two. Voice: the macOS voice Tara (`make.sh voice`), no paid service. Every video was built with `make.sh animate` followed by `make.sh voice`, so every one carries the animation layer. The library (`tools/`) was not changed in this pass; what it gets wrong is listed in section 4.

## 1. Result

- **33 of 33 videos built**, 33 pass every check: 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264 video and AAC audio, picture and sound equal within a tenth of a second (largest difference 0.011 s, V035), `animated` and `checks_passed` in every `VNNN.build.json`, the script hash of the build equal to the script on disk, no storyboard warning.
- Total length 8 h 59 min, 2.34 GB. Files: `video/production/out/VNNN.mp4`, with `.srt`, `.chapters.txt` and `.build.json` beside each.
- **25 videos were rebuilt after inspection** (31 builds; log `video/production/.cache/inspect-b2/rebuild.log` was deleted with the frame folder, the build lines are the same form as in `batch2-build.log`): V036 to V044, V048, V049, V051 to V054, V057 to V066. Not rebuilt: V034, V035, V045, V046, V047, V050, V055, V056.
- After the last edit `python3 tools/inject.py --check` reports 0 problems for every edited script and `python3 tools/check_course.py` reports 0 problems.

### How it was inspected, and by whom

- 1,085 frames were extracted from the finished MP4s (22 to 40 per video, never fewer than 22): the last moment of every paragraph read over an explainer scene, the middle of every long scene, the end of every line-drawn picture, the middle of every thinking pause and silent hold, and six spread over the length. Each frame was listed with its time, the script line, the stage directions before it and the narration at that moment.
- **First look: eight read-only helper agents** (four or five videos each) looked at every frame on contact sheets of six and opened doubtful ones at full size. They reported about 60 defects and 110 minor findings.
- **Second look: mine.** I opened at full or half size every finding that I acted on or that names a library defect (about 45 frames of the original builds), and after every rebuild the frames of every scene that was changed (about 75 frames of the rebuilt videos). Findings that I did not open myself are passed on as reported and are marked "reported" in the table.
- In a rebuilt video only the changed scenes were looked at again. The other scenes come from the same script lines and the same library and were not looked at a second time.
- **Not done:** no video was watched or listened to from start to end. Listening is not possible for me: the pronunciation of the computer voice was not heard. What the voice is given was read instead (section 5). `make.sh selftest` was not run, because it renders and another queue was rendering on the machine.

"Explainer scenes" in the table is the share of the running time during which a library scene or an animated commit graph is on screen, counted as in the batch 1 report.

### Imperfections that apply to several videos

- **Edges close to IDs.** A branch that the notation draws below its parent sends its edge past the right end of the parent's ID. Where it ran through characters or chips the graph was redrawn (15 graphs in 12 videos, with `^chain` and `dx=`). Where it only touches the last character it was left: V038, V041, V042, V052 to V056, and in the small panels of V040.
- **A stale terminal title** on a card that shows one command before a prediction (V034, V037, V050): defect 1.
- **The interview question is shown twice**, the second time cut short, in most videos (seen in V034; reported in V035, V038, V040 to V044, V052, V058 to V060, V062, V063): defect 7.
- **A recap that replays a scene with `replay:` is cut before its last step** (V056 twice, V057): defect 11.
- **Pauses held over the previous picture.** A prediction or "try it now" that follows a paragraph without a picture of its own is held over whatever was on screen (V036, V037, V040, V042, V056, V061). None of the sampled pauses shows its answer, except by elimination in V065.
- **Three steps in a short paragraph do not fit.** A scene that grows, labels and then plays a step needs about six seconds; in a shorter paragraph the scene is cut or ends in the next one (fixed in V039, V058, V059, V060, V066 with `at_<step>=` or a longer paragraph; left in V064 at 4:32).
- **Explainer share** runs from 14 % (V062) to 32 % (V048); 29 of the 33 are at 21 % or more.
