## 7. Each check and the way it can mislead

- **Spot.** CONCEPT, "**The limits of the checks.** Two,".
- **Picture.** Two gauges.
- **Steps.**
  1. Patch comparison (`--cherry-pick`): says "missing" for a fix that a human ported with a conflict resolution. Evidence then: "the `-x` line".
  2. Ancestry test: says "included" after an upward merge "resolved by discarding the release branch's side". Evidence then: `git log --remerge-diff`.
