Spots are given as section heading plus the first six words of the paragraph in `video/scripts/V196-*.md`. Every label is copied from the script. This is a briefing: no picture may show, paraphrase or hint at an item of the final test. Only the rules, the counts and the scoring that the script states are drawn.
