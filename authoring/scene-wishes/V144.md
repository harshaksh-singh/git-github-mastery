## 4. CONCEPT, "`GITHUB_OUTPUT`: you append `name=value`. The step," through "`GITHUB_STEP_SUMMARY`: you append Markdown, and it's"

**Picture.** Four labelled files handed to a step by the runner, each with what goes in and who gets it.

| File | You append | Effect | Limit from the script |
|---|---|---|---|
| `GITHUB_OUTPUT` | `name=value` | "an output of that name under the `steps` context" (the step "needs an `id`") | "1 MB per job and 50 MB per run" |
| `GITHUB_ENV` | `NAME=value` | "Later steps of the job get the environment variable. The writing step itself doesn't." | cannot override names that start with `GITHUB_` or `RUNNER_` |
| `GITHUB_PATH` | a directory | "prepended to `PATH` for later steps" | |
| `GITHUB_STEP_SUMMARY` | Markdown | "shown on the run's summary page" | "1 MiB per step, and 20 step summaries are shown per job" |
