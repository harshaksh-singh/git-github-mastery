# Scene wishes for V138: What a signature covers, what verification proves, and author spoofing

One library scene was placed: a two-state `graph` for the last demo step (`integration` fast-forwards from `4306257` to the signed tip `4687f7f` and takes the unsigned `bc6a046` along). Everything else needs pictures the library does not have yet: a commit card whose lines can be marked "signed" or "not signed", and a per-commit mark for the result letter and the signer. All labels are copied from the script. `signature-scope` and `spoof-signed` are volatile: the IDs and key fingerprints are those of the run shown in the script.

## 2. CONCEPT, "**Precisely.** `git commit` takes the author" and "The transport authenticates the pusher. It"

**Picture.** Where the two identity lines come from, and what the transport looks at.

1. Three sources feed the `author` line of a commit card: `GIT_AUTHOR_NAME` and `GIT_AUTHOR_EMAIL`; `--author`; `user.name` and `user.email`. Two feed `committer`: the `GIT_COMMITTER` variables; the same configuration.

4. Three small cards follow through the same gate: "a merge", "a rebase", "a cherry-pick" ("pushing other people's commits").

## 3. CONCEPT, "**What a signature covers.** Everything in" through "The push isn't covered. Who pushed"; DIAGRAM, "Check your paper. The tree and"
