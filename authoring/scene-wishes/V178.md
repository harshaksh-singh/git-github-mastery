## 4. Three defensible designs

- **Spot.** CONCEPT, "**Defensible designs,** from least to most" and the three numbered paragraphs.
- **Picture.** Three lanes, "from least to most machinery".
- **Lanes.**
  1. "do not evaluate fork pull requests automatically": on `pull_request`, the secret-free checks ("lint, unit tests, `ci/check.sh`, evaluation against a local stub or recorded responses"); then a maintainer reads the diff and triggers the paid evaluation by hand.
  2. "evaluate after merge": the evaluation runs on `main`; "alert or revert on regression".
  3. "separate data from code": a trusted workflow reads prompts and configuration "as data" and runs "the base repository's own evaluation code". - **Beside the lanes.** The evaluation's own key with "a spend cap": "a bill with a ceiling, not production traffic".
